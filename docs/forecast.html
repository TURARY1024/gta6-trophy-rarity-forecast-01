Method

Short version — the full write-up is in METHODOLOGY.md. Each rate is a ratio, earners over players tracked, and the two sides are forecast separately as log-linear trends in log-time, with trophy-class priors (story chapters rise steeply, 250-stunt grinds barely move) and a saturating fit that stops a rate's growth bending toward its ceiling. The answer is the page at the target as of Exophase's last refresh before it, so the forecast is evaluated half a refresh interval early. Spread combines the fits' prediction intervals, a horizon random-walk floor, a refresh-jump term and a flat allowance for model error.
